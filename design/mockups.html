PocketLedger — 4 layouts × 4 themes

Local-only Android app: scans bank SMS on-device, records every debit & credit, keeps your exact balance across accounts. Two independent choices: a layout (structure & navigation) and a theme (pure skin — palette, fonts, corners). Any theme works with any layout; theme switching is a settings toggle.
